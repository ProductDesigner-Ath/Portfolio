## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters and prospective freelance clients review this portfolio at a desk or on a phone. They need to quickly understand Atharv's visual craft, product reasoning, and practical use of AI in a real client engagement.

### Brand Personality
Restrained, editorial, assured. The portfolio should feel deliberate and authored, with a quiet technical precision rather than a generic product-case-study template.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing atharv.space visual language: tinted off-white surfaces, dark ink typography, fine rules, monospace utility labels, and cover art as the primary colour. The Ramdoot case study follows its approved Figma structure while remaining visually at home in the portfolio.

### Design Principles
1. Let the work and the decision-making lead; decoration supports both.
2. Use motion to explain navigation and image focus, with WebGL reserved for the signature gallery transition.
3. Preserve reading comfort through clear hierarchy, capped line lengths, and responsive single-column mobile composition.
4. Make claims precise: mark current features as in development and distinguish qualitative learning from metrics.
5. Make image inspection direct: one selected image, centred, with an obvious keyboard and touch-safe exit.
